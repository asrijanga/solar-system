## W1 · Inventory

`docs/data/mercury.md`: every dataset for Mercury's elevation, albedo, imagery, photometry, geometry and names.
- **Reachability:** each dataset checked from the cloud on 2026-10-05; all are reachable.
- **Conventions:** read from the files' own headers, PDS3 labels and the MDIS archive's own documentation (`MDIS_CDR_RDRSIS.PDF`).

**What it found:**
- **Three reference spheres:** 2439.4 km (USGS DEM and every MDIS map), 2440.0 km (MLA) and pck00011's 2440.53 × 2438.26 km ellipsoid. They differ by up to 1.13 km and must be reconciled before any blend.
- **Every MDIS map is normalised to i = 30°, e = 0°, g = 30°,** with the angles taken on the equipotential surface. Shading from slopes therefore stays in maps made with the Sun low:
  - The BDR (incidence near 74°) and HIE/HIW (78–86°) are rejected for albedo.
  - The MD3 and MDR colour maps (low-incidence campaigns, averaged over many images) are the candidates. Their residual shading is to measure against slopes from the DEM before W3 chooses.
- **Mercury's brightness:** Mallama & Hilton (2018) Eq. 2 (phase 2.1–169.5°) and Mallama et al. (2017) Table 7 (V 0.142) were read.
  - Domingue et al. (2016), whose law the maps were corrected with, has no open copy. The law will be fitted to Eq. 2 as Mars's was.
- **Checksums:** the PDS copies publish MD5s for every file; the USGS GeoTIFFs do not. Downloads use the PDS copies, or pin the USGS ones at first retrieval.
