Solar System

It is a period of quiet discovery. From a small blue world, a species barely two hundred thousand years old has begun to map the space around its star.

Robotic emissaries have crossed the dark to photograph every planet in the system. The pictures you are about to see are theirs. None of it is invented.

The worlds ahead sit where they truly sit tonight, and at their true size. Which means the Earth beneath you is about to become a single point of light….
